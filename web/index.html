How to Play Texas Hold’em

You're at the table with Dusty, Miss Lula Mae and Cody. Make the best five-card poker hand from your two hole cards and the five shared community cards, or get everyone else to fold.

  1. Blinds. The two players left of the dealer button post the small blind (10) and big blind (20) so there's something to play for. The button moves one seat each hand.
  2. Hole cards. Everyone gets two private cards, then the first betting round starts left of the big blind.
  3. The flop, turn and river. Three community cards come out (the flop), then one (the turn), then one more (the river), with a betting round after each.
  4. Your options. Check to pass when there's no bet to you. Bet to put chips in first. Call to match the current bet. Raise to increase it. Fold to give up the hand. This is no-limit: you can bet any amount up to all your chips (all-in).
  5. Showdown. If more than one player is left after the river, hands are shown and the best hand wins the pot. Every hand is named on screen so you can follow along. If someone is all-in, side pots are worked out automatically.

Your chips are saved between sessions. If you run out, the house restocks you for free. These chips have no cash value.
