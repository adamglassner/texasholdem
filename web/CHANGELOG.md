# Changelog

All notable changes to Texas Hold'em are recorded here.
Versions follow Semantic Versioning (MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-26

### Added
- First release of Texas Hold'em for Windows.
- No-limit Hold'em against three computer opponents at the Pecos Creek Saloon: Dusty Walker, Miss Lula Mae and Cody Briggs, each with their own style of play.
- Small and big blinds (10 / 20), hole cards, the flop, the turn and the river, with the dealer button moving every hand.
- Check, Bet, Raise, Call and Fold, with Min, Half Pot, Pot and All-in shortcuts and a bet slider.
- The pot, the current bet and every player's chip count are shown at all times, plus each player's last action and a Saloon Log of the hand.
- Side pots are worked out automatically when someone is all-in, and the cards go face up when the betting is over.
- At showdown every hand is named (Two Pair, Flush, Full House and so on) and the winning five cards are highlighted. A hand helper names your own best hand as the cards come out.
- Hand Rankings and How to Play guides for new players.
- Play-money chips only: no real money, no purchases and no accounts. Start with 2,000 chips, and the house restocks you for free if you run out.
- Your chips and stats persist between sessions, kept in %APPDATA%\Ironite\TexasHoldem. A hand interrupted by closing the game is called off and your chips are returned.
- Warm saloon setting: plank walls, lantern light, and a wood-rimmed table with a tooled-leather top.
- HD western deck drawn in-house: rancher kings, saloon-owner queens, cowhand jacks and a tooled-leather card back.
- Three original country tunes: "Pecos Creek Waltz", "Bluebonnet Two-Step" and "Lantern Light", with volume control and mute.
- Sound effects for the deal, chip bets, checks, folds, all-ins and a chime when the pot is won.
