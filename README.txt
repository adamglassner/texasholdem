TEXAS HOLD'EM - Pecos Creek Saloon  (v@VERSION@)
An Ironite game. (c) 2026 Ironite.
==================================================

Open Texas Hold'em from the Start menu (Ironite folder), your desktop, or
the Ironite Launcher. The game opens in its own window using Microsoft
Edge (built into Windows 10 and 11).

PLAY MONEY ONLY. Chips have no cash value. No real money, no purchases,
no accounts. You start with 2,000 chips and the house restocks you for
free if you run out.

HOW TO PLAY
- No-limit Hold'em against Dusty Walker, Miss Lula Mae and Cody Briggs.
  Blinds are 10 and 20, and the dealer button moves every hand.
- Make the best five-card hand from your two hole cards and the five
  community cards (the flop, the turn and the river).
- Check (K), Bet (B), Call (C), Raise (R) or Fold (F). Use the slider or
  the Min, Half Pot, Pot and All-in buttons to size your bet.
- At showdown every hand is named and the winning cards light up. Side
  pots are handled automatically when someone is all-in.
- Enter = next hand, M = music on/off, F11 = full screen.

Your chips, stats and settings are saved in %APPDATA%\Ironite\TexasHoldem,
outside the install folder, so updating or reinstalling keeps them.

All card art, music and sounds are original and made by the game.
